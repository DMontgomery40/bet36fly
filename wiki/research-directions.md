# Research directions after the associative experiments

This is the organized synthesis of Claude's September 13–14 research and David's additions. The [original memo](../docs/ASSOCIATIVE_NEXT_APPROACHES.md) remains intact, including its earlier ordering; this page uses its later ordering and combines overlapping proposals. [Reassessment](reassessment.md) owns measured status, [continual learning](continual-learning.md) owns the recovery/interference results, and [visual and downstream pathways](cells/visual-and-downstream.md) owns anatomical counts and source precedents.

These are proposals, not implemented experiments or authorization to launch them. The memo was written **before a recorded 2018 result**. Its expected verdict is not a confirmation measurement. The [integration record](../docs/evidence/wiki-integration-2026-09-14/reconciliation.md) identifies the source cutoff and what was actually checked.

## What the current results constrain

The current learner receives arbitrary team-odor identities and delayed winner reinforcement. Redundancy with Elo/form/win-rate features, cross-cue interference and a narrow two-channel readout are plausible explanations for its weak incremental performance. The experiments do **not** prove that it is mathematically just a running win counter, that a failed conventional model proves no signal exists, or that a richer readout cannot help. Finite negative tests constrain their tested information sets, representations and estimators.

A useful successor must test a particular missing computation or information source against matched controls. Giving the circuit a forecast through another sensory channel does not establish independent prediction. A fixed circuit can still be tested for useful nonlinear or temporal computation on the **same** information; it need not receive extra information to outperform a particular linear comparator.

## Proposed ordering and finite questions

The A–G order preserves the expanded memo's ranking. Its numerical novelty, cost and “probability of value” scores were qualitative judgments, not calibrated probabilities or measured runtimes. The early conventional-data screen and physiological calibration support the sequence without becoming extra overlapping architecture projects.

### A. Read what exists before collecting more

Every checked 2022 recovery arm contains 2,429 rows, with two summed MBON counts per team (`home_response`, `away_response`) and 179 daily checkpoint files. Those rows do **not** contain the broader neural state vectors requested in the memo. Conditioning records provide richer KC/MBON/DAN samples, but not every central-complex or downstream target. [Storage audit](../docs/evidence/wiki-integration-2026-09-14/anatomy-and-storage.json).

A wider readout therefore needs new plasticity-off probes of saved checkpoints with an explicitly wider sample, unless another saved recording is located. This would reuse development outcomes without new reinforcement, but still incur neural simulation. Claude's estimate of roughly 6,400 probes and one hour per arm is a planning estimate, not a measured budget. Failure of one fixed channel set would stop that bounded test, not prove that every unobserved neural variable lacks information.

### B. Preserve temporal state deliberately

The proposed reservoir input is a sequence of pregame-available recent games, margins, opponent strength, rest/travel, pitcher or team state where available, and uncertainty. All arms must receive identical availability-filtered information. Match state dimension and appropriate dynamical scale for the conventional/null reservoirs; predeclare which properties the rewiring preserves.

Current associative calls reset voltage, synaptic state, delayed events and refractory clocks. A reservoir test must encode its sequence within an explicit continuous call or implement and verify state carryover; persistent gains alone do not supply temporal electrical memory. The graph contains heterogeneous connectivity and sign proxies, but the present LIF constants and transmission delay are uniform. No heterogeneous-delay mechanism should be inferred from the memo's reservoir language. [Inspected state contract](associative-learning.md#signals-units-and-persistent-state).

### C. Change the reinforcement question, one mechanism at a time

Three related hypotheses belong together but require distinct tests: outcome surprise through MBON→DAN feedback; positive/negative residuals through justified reward/punishment compartments; and compartments with different memory timescales. Bennett's feedback model and Aso/Rubin's compartment-specific learning provide precedents, not already implemented functions. [Source ledger](sources.md#associative-learning-and-future-pathways--september-14).

An engineered residual can be `outcome − frozen pregame probability`, with the rule for sign and magnitude declared before testing. A non-neural online residual learner must receive the same team identity and delayed residual stream. If that learner fails, there is no demonstrated signal under that test; it is not proof that the residual is pure noise. A PPL1 punishment path needs its own link/valence checks. Anatomical feedback cannot simply be labeled a working reward-prediction-error circuit.

### D. Separate specificity from recovery

The retired-cue result motivates saved-artifact analyses of team-cue KC overlap, the number of cues sharing each eligible edge, and unintended response/gain change. Sparser codes, less-overlapping cue families, compartment separation and multiple timescales are possible remedies. Narrower odor codes previously missed MBON05; increasing rate to compensate is an unqualified proposal requiring a new physiological and stability check. High ρ makes many responses shallower, which does not itself improve credit assignment. [Measured interference and limitations](continual-learning.md#a-retired-cue-becomes-more-depressed-not-forgotten).

### E–G. Keep visual encoding, visual memory and choice separate

Visual input offers three distinct questions: fixed nonlinear encoding (memo 2/11A), associative memory (1/11B), and comparison/steering (3/11C/14). Each needs a specified stimulus-to-cell map and measured downstream response. A visual image of Elo is still Elo. The fixed-encoding arm needs a nonlinear comparator on the same rendering; the memory arm needs a frozen twin; the choice arm needs simultaneous alternatives and swap-reversing output. [Biology, exact anatomy and limits](cells/visual-and-downstream.md).

## Supporting work, with its own interpretation

| Direction | Connection to the sequence | Finite boundary |

| Physiology-derived calibration (13) | Address ALLN/APL/sign and dynamical limitations before unfamiliar visual/CX routes | Bind recordings, cell identity and measurement window; freeze parameters before sports evaluation |
| Task-fitted circuit parameters (6) | A separate machine-learning-with-anatomy approach | Disclose externally fitted signs/gains/time constants, compare to conventional models and prior v1/v2 results; do not call it dopamine learning |

Lappalainen's unknown parameters were optimized for motion detection and then compared with physiology; that is not the same procedure as fitting directly to recordings. Both can inform calibration design, but their evidence must remain distinct. Source leads include Shiu for the inherited LIF baseline; Honegger/Turner/Hige for KC/MBON physiology; Zhao/Cameron for sensory-rate anchors; and DoOR/Hallem for receptor-specific odor responses. The [sensory page](natural-sensory-inputs.md) retains their exact units and access limits.

## Data-use boundary

2022 is development; 2023 is an already exposed, completed sensory confirmation. The 2018 associative confirmation was reported launched once in Claude's session; this synthesis neither launches nor repeats it and does not infer its result. Do not call 2018 unused based on older handoff wording. Earlier seasons are candidate sources only: audit previous access and chronological training relationships before declaring any block unused. Mechanism and anatomy tests need no sports confirmation block; a new predictive claim needs an appropriately frozen, independently evaluated design. [Evidence gates](evidence-gates.md).
