## Exact update order and numeric state

Retain all electrical scheduling, refractory handling, sampled counts, random draws and transmission ordering. At each active step, after actual spikes are detected, inject K_j/tau_r and D_c/tau_r into double rates. K_j is the actual binary KC event; D_c is actual population-mean DAN count, formed using double division. Scheduled pulse requests are not events for the learning formula. Eligibility is its prior value at this left boundary; it does not receive the new impulse directly.

Let r=1/tau_r, e=1/tau_e, b=r+e, A=−expm1(−b*h)/b. For each eligible edge, Q=E_D*R_K−E_K*R_D and attempted delta=eta*n*A*Q. Integrate each eligibility exactly using the same post-injection rates: E_next=exp(−e*h)*E+R*(exp(−r*h)−exp(−e*h))/(e−r); then R_next=exp(−r*h)*R. Use numerically stable exponential differences. There is one time integral, no extra factor h, and no slow weight-tracking state.

Rates and eligibility are double. Add a double bounded gain accumulator for each eligible edge, initialized once per electrical trial from its saved float32 gain. Let G be this accumulator and F the published float32 gain. On every interval: proposed=G+attempted; G_next=clamp(proposed); F_next=float32(G_next). Transmission on subsequent steps uses F_next under the unchanged electrical ordering. Never replace G_next with the rounded F_next during the trial. This is numeric accumulation, not a new slow physiological weight state.

Record three distinct deltas: attempted double delta; bounded accumulator delta G_next−G; published change double(F_next)−double(F). Published changes telescope to the actual checkpoint difference. A sub-ULP interval may have zero published change while its double accumulation persists; many such intervals must eventually publish their accumulated effect. Do not identify zero published single-step change with zero learning.

Pre-onset rates/eligibility stay exactly zero and pre-onset events are excluded. Each call starts electrical and bridge states at zero. After the trial and analytic drain, publish the final float32 checkpoint and discard rates, eligibility and the sub-ULP double remainder. Across trials only the declared float32 gains persist. Checkpoint load/clone follows this same rule. Excluded edges remain byte-identical and continue transmitting with their saved gain. Disabled plasticity must retain all gain bytes, including across the tail; effective-eta-zero attempts are zero rather than hidden hypothetical configured-eta updates.

## Analytic no-new-event continuation

After the last electrical interval has advanced R/E to the exact electrical endpoint, compute Q_end and tail attempted=eta*n*Q_end/b. Apply that one monotone integral to the current double accumulator, clamp, and publish once more. This analytically integrates learning signals conditioned on no further events; it is not another neural run, a measured silent brain, or a shifted observation window. No count, membrane voltage or spike bin extends beyond the electrical endpoint.

Record tail effects as a separate `analytic_no_new_event_tail` phase. Never put them into the last electrical bin or silently omit them from total gain reconciliation. The three existing electrical phases keep their boundaries. Total attempted, bounded-double and published changes each equal electrical-phase sums plus the separately named tail. The diagnostic's final gain changes, teaching contrasts, cumulative sequence and zero-bound gate must include the tail. Raw-event modes have no such added continuation and retain their historical assertions.

Bounds are observations of the state actually used as well as the accumulator: an interval low/high observation occurs when its double proposal is at/beyond the corresponding bound OR its published float32 value is at that bound. This prevents rounding to a transmitted boundary from escaping a double-only check. Count at most once per edge/side/interval. Tail observations are one separately labeled analytic-segment observation per eligible edge/side, not an invented infinite count of dwell steps. Check the final saved eligible gains independently too. Frozen dwelling and equality retain inclusive semantics; excluded edges remain excluded.

## Recording contract

Common spike/count/pulse metadata may remain shared. Event-specific rule arrays retain their old semantics and shape under event mode. Under bridge mode they must be absent/non-applicable or explicitly labeled shadow event diagnostics; in particular an old `applied` field must never appear to reconstruct the bridge when it does not. Consumers dispatch on the explicit learning rule and layout version, not an inferred presence of arrays.

Use these optional double arrays for bridge mode, with shape and ordered field labels stored in metadata:

- `bridge_signals[step,compartment,2]`: post-injection R_D and prior E_D, the values used for this interval. Units spikes/ms and spikes.
- `bridge_kc_bins[bin,kc,2]`: end-of-bin R_K and E_K, with the same units.
- `bridge_kc_used[step,group,2]`: post-injection R_K mass and prior E_K mass summed over eligible edges in that group, including edge multiplicity. This permits reconstruction with compartment signals when each group belongs to one compartment. Mixed-compartment groups are permitted only with their explicit mapping/limitations; their per-edge summed Q remains authoritative.
- `bridge_rule[step,group,8]`: positive integrated term; negative integrated term; attempted net; bounded-double applied; published-float change; inclusive low observations; inclusive high observations; Q summed over eligible edges at the interval start.
- `bridge_tail[group,8]`: the same fields for the analytic continuation, with Q taken after the final electrical advance.

Positive/negative terms need careful naming. Although A*Q is the exact net integral, A*E_D*R_K and −A*E_K*R_D individually omit equal rate-driven contributions. To record actual separate integrals use C=[−expm1(−2*r*h)/(2*r)−A]/(e−r). Positive=eta*n*(A*E_D*R_K+C*R_D*R_K); negative=−eta*n*(A*E_K*R_D+C*R_K*R_D). Their sum is the attempted net. For the infinite tail use A_tail=1/b and C_tail=1/(2*r*b). Compute the net directly from A*Q to avoid unnecessary cancellation; verify decomposition numerically. These terms have gain units; Q has spikes squared/ms under the declared normalization.

Record per-edge or per-group trial totals of attempted, bounded-double, published, clipping discrepancy and final rounding discrepancy, and retain the actual final gain vector. Group totals are sufficient if eligibility/edge mapping is retained. At no clipping, attempted and bounded-double sums agree to double arithmetic error; published-versus-double endpoint discrepancy is bounded by the final float32 rounding error, not one ULP multiplied by all time steps. If frozen, attempted and both applied changes are zero even if R/E/Q are nonzero.

Record-only buffers must not influence dynamics or become the only storage needed for the accumulator/tail. Record on/off results must be byte-identical for published gains, spikes, voltages and counts. Allocation budgets must include all new buffers; retain raw-mode allocation behavior.

## Independent tests before any circuit panel

Keep every existing raw and tonic test unchanged and explicitly selected. The new bridge tests must use independent analytic pair sums or high-accuracy continuous integration, not another copy of the native recurrence as the sole oracle.

1. For isolated unit pairs and full tail, expected change is −eta*(exp(−lag/500)−exp(−lag/100)) for positive KC-first lag; reverse sign for DAN-first, zero for coincidence. Test the frozen signed lags 0, ±0.2, ±1, ±5, ±20, ±50, ±100, ±500, ±1000 ms. Test populations 1/2/22, synchronous replicated populations and partial population activity to distinguish population mean from sum. Confirm actual spikes in all native fixtures.
2. Use double-attempted/accumulator oracle tolerance absolute 1e-11 per isolated unit edge and relative 1e-8 for larger unclipped sums. Final published gain must agree with float32(initial+analytic effect), allowing at most one float32 ULP plus the double-reference tolerance. Do not require a 99% relative storage floor for an effect intrinsically below one ULP. Preserve the predeclared nonvanishing floors at well-resolved lags: 500 ms at least 95% of the raw effect and 50 ms at least 25%. For many small intervals, explicitly demonstrate that nonzero total learning survives publication despite individually sub-ULP changes.
3. For arbitrary finite spike histories without clipping, compare to an independent all-pairs sum of that analytic kernel. Cover multiple/repeated/simultaneous events, empty/KC-only/DAN-only sequences, masks, both compartments/KC families and permuted edge ordering. Large-lag sign/decay, antisymmetry and coincidence follow from this oracle.
4. Verify end-time independence by appending silent electrical intervals before analytic drain with exactly the same actual event history. Shift events and onset together; final gains must remain unchanged when their relative timings are unchanged. Onset exclusion must test just-before/exactly-at/just-after onset, and gains must include tail-only changes even if the electrical interval ended immediately after the second event.
5. Verify the signal integrator at reference grids 0.1/0.2/0.4 ms with common representable event times, using an independent reference, while public native electrical dt remains 0.2. Do not present this as a convergence test of the entire LIF circuit at varied dt. Under clipping, use a separately integrated high-accuracy reference because unclipped pair superposition is no longer sufficient.
6. Test lower/upper exact contact, strict overshoot, published-only boundary contact due to rounding, contact followed by recovery, tail-only contact and frozen dwelling. Verify finite/tail counts separately and their overall gate inclusion. Zero-effective-eta/no-eligible-edge paths must preserve gains. Test masks and checkpoint/reset/clone/trial transitions, including discarded double remainders and retained published gains.
7. Verify recording decompositions: positive+negative equals attempted, Q coefficient equals attempted, electrical phases+tail equal totals, and published totals equal actual saved gains minus initial gains. Test record-on/off parity and malformed configuration/buffer-size rejection across both old and new mode paths.

These numeric tolerances arise from double arithmetic and float32 publication, not any measured held-out response. If numerical tests cannot meet them, diagnose arithmetic/units or justify a changed numerical contract before new measurement; do not shrink eta or omit tail effects to pass.
