# Dopamine signal source investigation, resumed

Checked 2026-09-13 UTC (2026-09-12 local). Scope: source reading, current implementation inspection and static anatomical analysis. No new circuit run, production change, source-data change, or learning result. This report closes the bounded source investigation; it does not close SCI-001.

The strongest concrete mismatch is that the implementation assigns one population-mean DAN signal to every eligible edge in a channel, while experiments resolve local signaling along KC axons. Retained pair counts cannot yet assign that local signal. A second, separately testable hypothesis is a causal, rectified **rate** contrast signal. That is mathematically distinct from the rejected signed event-baseline rule. A fixed engineered version is specified below, but the inspected fly sources do not establish exact adaptation or its 500 ms timescale in these selected cells. Neither hypothesis justifies changing the home mask or relaxing the guard.

## What the code actually supplies

Inspected `reward_lif.cpp`, construction in `reward_protocol.py`, and allocations/native invocation in `reward_brain.py`; hashes accompany the reading receipt. Actual DAN spikes arise from the electrical graph and teaching pulses. Teaching labels are not themselves a plasticity gate. Pure dopamine outgoing fast weights are zero; the selected 24 DANs affect plasticity through two channel signals. One KC trace is shared across its eligible edges. Home pools two PPL101 cells; away pools all 22 PAM12 cells. MBON spiking does not enter the learning equation, though recurrent pathways can affect DAN firing.

There is no per-synapse release, local dopamine concentration, receptor occupancy, cAMP/IP3/ER state, or receptor-specific learning law. Available annotation columns do not fill these gaps: selected DAN `receptorType` and `rootSide` values are null. Existing family differences are anatomical masks and channel membership, not different receptor equations. Absence of those mechanisms is a simulator gap, not evidence that biological learning cannot work.

The raw event rule has opposing prior-trace/event products. The bridge instead filters nonnegative KC/DAN events with 100 ms rates and 500 ms eligibility, integrates their opposing products, and accounts for the complete no-new-event tail. Neither is the full Jiang model. Bridge rate/eligibility history begins at the 100 ms learning onset; only gains persist between calls. The rejected onset-history shadow changes this history boundary and worsens 30 of 32 saved trials. It cannot rule out nonlinear signal generation, but does rule out promoting onset history alone as the repair.

The second-panel untaught-home guard still fails in both corrected raw and bridge runs. Static counts and delayed gain publication do not establish which input caused that failure. Global positive rescaling generally rescales both mean and standard deviation and cannot cure their ratio without a separate nonlinear effect.

## A fixed engineered rate-adaptation hypothesis

This is a **candidate for an explicitly engineered contrast detector**, not a source-derived cellular law and not an assertion that background dopamine is irrelevant. Lack of biological calibration does not prevent a testable engineering hypothesis. The positive-only choice deliberately cannot reproduce the natural PAM-γ3 suppression signal described in the source ledger.

The rejected event rule subtracts an estimated events-per-cell-per-step baseline from each spike bin, including empty bins. Rectifying those isolated impulses mostly leaves spike bins intact. Filtering before comparison is different: an adaptive reference can follow sustained activity while preserving transient increases. The distinction is mathematical, not evidence of efficacy.

One fully fixed continuous model is:

```text
S_Kj(t) = sum of actual KC spike impulses
S_Dc(t) = sum of actual selected-DAN impulses / channel population size
dR_Kj/dt = (S_Kj - R_Kj) / 100 ms
dR_Dc/dt = (S_Dc - R_Dc) / 100 ms
dB_Dc/dt = (R_Dc - B_Dc) / 500 ms
Dplus_c = max(R_Dc - B_Dc, 0)
dE_Kj/dt = R_Kj - E_Kj / 500 ms
dE_Dc/dt = Dplus_c - E_Dc / 500 ms
dg_jc/dt = 0.0005 * 0.96 * (E_Dc * R_Kj - E_Kj * Dplus_c)
```

Rates have spikes/ms, eligibility has spike units, and impulse integration injects `1/100 ms` per KC event or the population-normalized equivalent. All states start at zero at electrical time zero and follow actual activity throughout the call; gain writes begin at the unchanged 100 ms onset. Both signed-product terms use the **same** Dplus signal, including its eligibility. Apply the existing gain bounds and eligible mask. Continue all filters with zero new input after the existing electrical endpoint and integrate their entire tail. Discard dynamic states after publishing the final checkpoint; preserve only gains, as in the present trial interface. Learning-off has no gain writes. No baseline uses future activity, a cue label, a teaching schedule, a selected outcome or a measured guard statistic.

Parameter provenance: 100 ms rate, 500 ms eligibility, eta, normalization, bounds, onset and trial policy are inherited engineering choices. The **new adaptation time equals the existing 500 ms eligibility time solely as a predeclared one-timescale engineering assumption**. The inspected primary sources do not identify this equality or an exact-adaptation law for these families. It adds no fitted response constant, but remains an uncalibrated new mechanism. It should not be described as a biological repair before evidence.

The old bridge analytic tail cannot be reused: rectification creates piecewise dynamics. Before any circuit implementation, an independent continuous integration oracle must resolve sign crossings, the full zero-input continuation and constrained gains. A fixed candidate also needs a frozen numerical publication contract; copying the old tail would evaluate a different equation. Sustained positive DAN input tends to Dplus=0, but stochastic positive excursions persist. A cue-evoked transient can still teach, and the rule can still depress untaught home input. There is no guaranteed guard improvement.

A bounded offline comparison on the already captured full spike histories could reject this equation without new electrical runs. It must preserve all trials and compare against the same-history unadapted bridge as well as the actual bridge; improvements are conditional replay evidence, since alternative gains have not influenced recurrent activity. Do not sweep adaptation times, rectification offsets or normalization after observing results. A promising shadow would require a separate preregistered real diagnostic identity, never immediate conditioning.

## Falsifiers and stop conditions

Numerical tests must cover constant input/adaptation, finite pulses, offset with nonnegative Dplus, positive and negative rate changes, same-time events, event order, pre-onset history, channels of unequal population size, exact mask preservation, learning-off parity, checkpoint/reset policy, sign crossings and complete tails. Use analytic constant/pulse limits or an independent high-accuracy ODE solution; implementation-mirroring recurrences are insufficient. Reject unexplained numerical residuals, dependence on metadata instead of actual spikes, gain changes on excluded edges, or a tail omitted to remove drift.

Physiological claims would require additional evidence beyond these engineering tests. A positive-only contrast signal cannot claim to explain natural γ3 reward suppression, and a single generic timing function cannot claim all γ1pedc and γ4 induction results. A localized coupling candidate must first demonstrate real contact territory, including apparently cross-side pairs and unsupported/ambiguous sites.

For either engineering candidate, both unchanged original and second diagnostic panels must pass all seven criteria with new identities and complete replay evidence before acquisition/reversal. Lower mean depression alone is insufficient if the mean/SD guard still fails, teaching separation collapses or cue specificity is lost. Conditioning must then demonstrate actual taught-response depression, discrimination, controls and recovery from acquired memory under the existing contract. Software tests, static anatomy and shadow replay remain separate evidence tiers.
