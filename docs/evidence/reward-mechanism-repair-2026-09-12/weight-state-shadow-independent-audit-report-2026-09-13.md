## Limits and next boundary

The 1e-11 gain allowance remains conditional, not a formal global ODE error
enclosure. Reference agreement is much tighter than that allowance but does
not change its logical status. The full saved arrays support domain, mask,
sum and four-endpoint checks on the counters; they do not retain every
intermediate double gain. Consequently this audit does not independently
reconstruct every transient bound or rounding observation. Those observations
come from the separately tested wrapper. Similarly, the 128 fixed references
do not constitute independent ODE evaluation of every edge.

The result is a valid failure of this specified weight-state law on these
captured untaught trajectories. It is not a full-circuit candidate, a local
anatomical exposure model, acquisition, reversal or improved prediction. The
audit performed zero network, native or circuit calls and made no production
or frozen-source changes. The current failed production qualification remains
unchanged; this rejection supplies no permission to loosen its guard or start
conditioning.
