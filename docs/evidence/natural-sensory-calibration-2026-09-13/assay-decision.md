## Conditional finite design, requiring a new resolved identity

This design specifies limits rather than inventing missing parameters. Before execution, replace every unresolved binding in a new immutable protocol: input body IDs, specimen transfer rationale, absolute baseline and stimulus schedule, stimulus/dose/solvent, adaptation approximation and its uncertainty, candidate behavioral readout and fixed interpretation. Require supported neutral, appetitive, aversive and mixed schedules. Geosmin and water rates and mixed-pattern interactions are currently unresolved. A source-measured window average may support an explicitly approximate constant schedule only with baseline and transfer assumptions declared; it does not become a measured waveform.

Freeze the full graph/source hashes; no edge pruning or anatomical side split. Disable all plasticity and learned decoders. Explicitly declare dynamics and any global synaptic conversion in the new protocol. The legacy constructor's ALPN incoming-zero, KC 1.25, APL outgoing 0.25, global gain and pure-DAN intervention must not enter implicitly. The current native reward input uses 0.2 ms Bernoulli steps, 68.75 mV events and designated-input refractory handling; these are implementation facts requiring an explicit sensory model choice, not physiology calibration.

After resolution, the proposed maximum is five conditions (null, solvent/neutral, appetitive, aversive, mixed) × three fixed seeds (17, 43, 101) = **15 trials**, plus one exact repeat = **16 calls**. Each trial resets membrane, synaptic/refractory state, random generator and any adaptation state; no history crosses trials. Reserve at most 60 minutes wall time and 2 seconds of prestimulus plus the source window plus 2 seconds recovery per trial (taste 7 s, odor 4.5 s). These are proposed observation bounds; if the physiological source requires more, revise and identify the protocol before running. Use the same seeds across conditions. Keep all source-window counts and rates per input and per output, not just a population aggregate.

Preflight must cover units, absolute versus delta baseline, timestep probability bounds, exact selected IDs, null input, generator saturation, deterministic replay and input-induced versus recurrent input spikes. Record requested events, delivered events, actual sensory spikes and downstream spikes separately. Compare measured-window output and matched neutral baseline, report all three seeds and uncertainty; do not call a model rate a natural recording. Establish numerical finite/bounded-state checks and source-specific expected response directions before seeing results. ORN-to-PN propagation alone has no appetitive/aversive sign expectation; a behavioral output requires its own evidence.

Stop at the earliest failed stage: unresolved identity/schedule; generator discrepancy beyond its predeclared stochastic bound; numerical instability/saturation; failed exact replay; missing intended sensory activation; absent downstream transmission; unsupported readout interpretation. Do not expand doses, retune gains, train a decoder or add molecular mechanisms within this identity. First-stage success would establish only the specified plasticity-off response and authorize consideration of a separate two-source protocol.
