# Two opportunities and pregame translation: prepared, not implemented

No two-source trial or sports pilot ran. First qualify the basic sensory assay. The initial comparison, if qualified, should probe each option separately with identical resets and common seeds and compare fixed output summaries **externally**. That comparison is engineered. A later in-circuit choice needs an explicit source-preserving biological circuit and temporal/spatial protocol. Neither an unlabeled odor mixture nor left/right ORN root labels identifies teams.

## Symmetric, absolute quality contract

For each team produce independent pregame quality `q` on a common training-frozen scale, an uncertainty interval, feature availability timestamps and provenance. Apply the same function to home and away; team identity cannot select a gain, sensory population, output sign or threshold. Home advantage can be a documented pregame covariate evaluated under the same rule. Do not scale the two values by their within-match minimum/maximum or complement one quality from the other.

The encoding function takes absolute quality and uncertainty and returns a supported stimulus/dose schedule only after calibration. Until then its schedule is **unbound**, not a fabricated Hz value. Absolute attractive/poor thresholds, interpolation domain and extrapolation behavior must be selected on training history and frozen before held-out outcomes. Contrast `q_home - q_away` and its uncertainty are additional descriptors; they cannot make an absolutely good opportunity bad solely because its opponent is better. A good/aversive pair requires independent support for the low quality plus a sufficiently certain large contrast. Wide intervals produce an explicit uncertain condition rather than endpoint forcing.

The following are synthetic quality-space acceptance examples, not physiological doses or probabilities:

| Example | Home / away qualities | Required representation after calibration |
|---|---|---|
| Equal/equal | 0.50 / 0.50 | Identical schedules at a common moderate quality |
| Good/good | 0.85 / 0.80 | Both attractive; retain the small difference |
| Poor/poor | 0.20 / 0.20 | Both low quality; no forced winner |
| Good/aversive | 0.90 / 0.10, narrow uncertainty | Distinct supported appetitive and aversive conditions if absolute thresholds support them |
| Uncertain contrast | 0.65 / 0.35, overlapping wide intervals | Preserve uncertainty; no reliable good/aversive claim |

Swap home/away labels and qualities and require swapped schedules/output summaries, not sign-specific retuning. Counterbalance A/B presentation order and common seeds. Run equal/equal, good/good, poor/poor and good/aversive with both orders only under a later finite protocol. For external reset probes, identical inputs must have identical seeded responses regardless of presentation label. If later using spatially distinct channels, verify side symmetry and independence anatomically and functionally before interpreting choice.

## Information and evaluation boundary

Only information published by the prediction timestamp may enter the encoder. Fit imputations, scaling, quality models, thresholds and sensory transformations on chronological training history. Lock the split and transform identity before held-out results. Keep source timestamps and missingness; do not backfill later lineups or outcomes as if pregame. No tuning on neural test responses or sports test labels.

If a predictor supplies sweetness/quality, preserve and score that exact encoder-only predictor. Also include a conventional baseline using the same pregame information, plus a constant/prior baseline. Compare the complete neural pipeline on the same fixtures and uncertainty procedure; following the injected forecast is not independent neural discovery. Any later sports probability readout must disclose fitting separately. Similar sensory responses do not imply a soccer draw or a calibrated probability. Palatability, acquired reward learning and predictive usefulness remain separate claims.

For later conditioning, separate pregame cues from post-outcome reinforcers and predeclare paired, timing-unpaired, untaught, frozen and reversal controls with a new identity. Preserve the failed legacy SCI-001 result; it is not a prerequisite for this frozen sensory assay.
