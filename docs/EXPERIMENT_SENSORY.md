# Natural sensory pipeline and better-than-chance goal

Status: active development; **no goal qualification yet**. Authorized September 13, 2026. The first calibration checkpoint remains an immutable zero-call result. This protocol extends execution, not the meaning of past evidence.

## Goal acceptance

Use MLB as the first confirmatory task because it has a binary outcome and ample chronological data. Soccer remains separate; similar sensory responses are never draws. A frozen pipeline must use the actual MaleCNS circuit with source-informed taste inputs, after sensory and two-probe acceptance. An independently fitted feature model without the circuit cannot complete this goal.

Report accuracy and log loss against both uniform binary chance and a training-frequency prior. The confirmation rule requires a positive accuracy margin against 0.5 and negative paired log-loss differences against uniform chance and the training prior, with one-sided block-bootstrap confidence bounds excluding zero. Resample complete ISO weeks, preserve paired predictions and include all eligible games. Use 10,000 bootstrap replicates, fixed seed 20260913 and alpha 0.025 for the first confirmation attempt. This is a conditional historical backtest, not evidence of profit or future reliability.

Report encoder-only, same-information conventional and circuit-silenced/appropriate ablation results on the same fixtures. A pipeline can beat chance while showing no incremental neural advantage; state that explicitly. No fitted readout may be called innate neural learning. Report sample size, dates, all predeclared metrics, uncertainty and failures, not only a successful sport/seed/slice.

## Confirmation protection

Existing 2024–August 2026 MLB data and historical v1/v2 results have already been inspected and are development evidence. Do not relabel them as new confirmation. Reserve a separate 2023 MLB regular-season confirmation block, with 2019–2021 history for training and 2022 for development. Before any new 2023 outcomes are fetched or read, freeze the candidate selection procedure, final pipeline and data eligibility contract. Development may use existing data plus the earlier training/development years; no 2024+ information may enter a 2023 prediction.

Fetch raw public results into a separate source directory, retain URLs/timestamps/hashes, use the existing conservative 48-hour result availability rule and freeze missingness/feature transforms on training only. Exclude non-final, tied, cancelled and non-regular-season games by rules fixed before retrieval. Duplicate IDs or inconsistent outcomes fail data validation. A failed confirmation is preserved and cannot be retried as a new independent test. Later attempts require unused confirmation observations and a new preregistered identity; allocate alpha 0.05/2^attempt_index across confirmation attempts.

## Finite stages

1. Reconcile the recovered Tastekin molecular table against exact native IDs and verify primary physiology. Declare any zero-background model assumption, window-average approximation and population transfer; do not label them measured baseline or waveform. Source-model sweeps stay distinct from physiological doses.
2. Freeze one plasticity-off assay with explicit graph/dynamics/input/readout identities, complete requested/achieved input and output instrumentation, matched null/neutral/appetitive/aversive/mixed controls, seeds and numerical checks. Each assay has its own finite call/time cap and stop at the earliest failing stage. Revisions require a measured limitation and a new rationale/protocol before rerunning.
3. Accept equal/equal, good/good, poor/poor and good/aversive cases plus swaps and order/reset controls. Initially compare independent exposures externally and label the comparison engineered. No pooled-mixture or hemisphere-based team-choice claim.
4. Freeze symmetric absolute-quality translation, uncertainty, training-only transforms and baseline comparators. Develop in finite candidate batches recorded before each batch. Never force the weaker of two good teams to an aversive endpoint.
5. Freeze a final candidate before confirmation access, execute once, report all controls and check goal acceptance. If not achieved, diagnose on development evidence and continue without rewriting failures or reusing the confirmation set as an adaptive stop.

Source retrieval and anatomical checks do not authorize an undeclared neural schedule. [Evidence and stage record](evidence/sensory-backtest-goal-2026-09-13/index.md). Detailed native assay and backtest configurations are frozen separately before execution. No active model promotion, legacy experiment resume, push or deployment.
