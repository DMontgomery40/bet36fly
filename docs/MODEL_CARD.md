## What is real, and what is modeled

The neuron IDs, anatomical edges and contact counts come from the official release. The retained graph contains 166,700 annotated neurons and 25,582,938 directed neuron-pair connections, representing 124,177,617 synaptic contacts. All released edges between retained neurons remain, including 10,299,701 weight-one edges and 101 self edges. Unresolved fragments and non-neuronal objects are excluded with exact accounting in the import manifest. The older female FlyWire release is the approximately 140,000-neuron brain; the September 2026 male release includes the ventral nerve cord.

The active v1 and v2 neuron dynamics use leaky integrate-and-fire equations and numerical parameters from Shiu et al., *A Drosophila computational brain model reveals sensorimotor processing* (Nature, 2024). This is an independent C++ implementation applied to the newer male graph, not a reproduction of their validated experiments on the female FlyWire graph. It advances all retained neurons and propagates spikes along their actual outgoing edges. The following numerical settings describe v1/v2; the separate reward protocol explicitly changes trial timing, global fast-weight scale and dopamine-only transmission.

Rest and reset voltage are -52 mV; threshold is -45 mV; membrane time constant 20 ms; synaptic decay 5 ms; refractory time 2.2 ms; transmission delay 1.8 ms; per-contact weight magnitude 0.275 mV. Exact subthreshold integration occurs between discrete 0.2 ms events. Each trial lasts 80 ms. V1 uses trial seed 42. V2 prespecifies seeds 42, 137 and 2026, with common stimulus randomness within each seed across compared variants. The chosen brief stimulus probes the transient response, not real-time cognition.

Presynaptic acetylcholine is modeled as excitatory; GABA, glutamate and histamine as inhibitory. Ambiguous, missing or modulator-only predictions use +1 so their edges remain active. This coarse proxy has 3,718 uncertain-sign neurons and does not reproduce receptor-specific physiology. Some model neurons fire at high rates; numerical activity is not proof of physiological validity.

## Inputs and outputs

Sixteen pregame features describe lagged team Elo, form, win rate, games played, rest and score margin, plus sport. Training-only scaling is saved in the checkpoint. Positive and negative feature channels drive 32 evenly spaced, real ALPN neurons from the 686-neuron annotated ALPN catalog, using artificial 30–270 Hz Poisson stimulation. Other ALPNs remain in the graph without external drive. The feature-to-neuron mapping is an artificial sensory interface; the active model does not read screenshots, understand team names, watch a browser, or receive outcome labels at inference. The separate reward experiment supplies historical outcome-dependent DAN pulses during training only. That experiment also uses its own sensory interface: a glomerular identity code in which each feature drives a tuned set of annotated cholinergic ALPN glomeruli, so which ports fire depends on the game ([reward protocol](EXPERIMENT_REWARD.md)).

The prediction readout receives actual firing rates from the 97 MBONs and average activity by neuronal superclass. All ALPN neurons are excluded from superclass readouts, preventing a direct sensory-feature shortcut. The artificial learned readout maps spikes to home/draw/away probabilities; the draw output is disabled for MLB.

## Active v1 training program

1. Run each historical pregame stimulus through the entire initial spiking brain and cache the resulting neural activity. The cache identity includes the graph, current synaptic weights, input matrix, neural code, seed, timestep and trial duration.
2. Train a differentiable rate surrogate to learn bounded positive multiplicative gains on the 61,210 existing Kenyon-cell→MBON synapses. Gains stay within exp(-0.3) and exp(0.3), preserve transmitter signs and cannot create new edges. This supervised surrogate is an engineering approximation, not biological dopamine learning or exact backpropagation through spikes.
3. Apply these learned gains to those actual synapses in the full 166,700-neuron spiking graph. Rerun the complete brain for every game. The surrogate is discarded for serving.
4. Fit the final sports readout on those resulting real spike counts. Select epochs using validation loss; save gains, input/output scaling and readout arrays. Every served pick runs this full trained spiking network and its final readout.

The exact learned-edge count, gain range, real spike changes, training curves and held-out metrics are generated by the run. Changed weights alone are not treated as evidence of improved prediction.

The decoder-only diagnostics verified the complete 8,075-row biological seed-42 cache and used only 4,447 training and 1,351 validation rows. Whole features are `log1p(mean(raw window rates))` with 124 channels; temporal features are window-first `log1p(raw window rates)` with 496. Scaling is fitted separately per representation on pooled training rows across sports, with standard-deviation floor 0.01 and clipping to [−8, 8]. The first diagnostic tried C = 0.01, 0.1 and 1.0; its preserved report records temporal’s disadvantage on that grid.

The authorized stronger-L2 extension changed only C to {0.00001, 0.0001, 0.001, 0.01}, after a required C=0.01 reproduction gate passed all four references. All 16 fits converged without captured warnings. Soccer selected C=0.001 for both representations: whole validation log loss 1.011525, temporal 1.002819. Baseball selected C=0.0001 for both: whole 0.688307, temporal 0.687752. Relative to reproduced C=0.01, temporal-minus-whole changed from +0.104550 to −0.008706 for soccer and +0.025153 to −0.000556 for baseball. The saved feature baseline remains better at 0.962021/0.682923; both selected readouts now beat the prior’s point estimates of 1.056507/0.690260.

This reversal shows sensitivity to regularization in this decoder, not an established root cause, independent confirmation or a reason to promote a model. C was selected on the same validation data after earlier results motivated the extension. No additional grid expansion is automatic. The tracked [development summary](evidence/v2-development-summary.md#decoder-diagnostics) contains the aggregate scores; the detailed report is a local artifact. Preprocessing is unchanged and finite: pooled temporal clipping is 0.055130%/0.050590% for train/validation entries versus 0.011425%/0.007163% whole, with constant-channel counts 85/86 versus 9/9. Those unchanged diagnostics are not proven causes.

Shared plasticity has 295 actual gain parameters: one per supported pair among 15 supplied KC type labels and 37 supplied MBON type labels. Annotation rows must match graph body IDs; broad `KC` and `KCg` labels are retained. Gains are shared across sports and hemispheres. Independent plasticity has 61,210 actual gain parameters. Unsupported positions are not parameters. Both use `gain = exp(0.3 × tanh(theta))` on supported edges and one elsewhere.

Whole-trial readout has 124 channels: 97 MBON rates followed by 27 superclass averages excluding all ALPNs. Temporal readout concatenates that same layout window-first for `[0,20)`, `[20,40)`, `[40,60)` and `[60,80)` ms, giving 496 channels. Counts in each window become Hz by multiplication by 50. Averaging the four windows recovers whole-trial rates. Decoder coefficients and biases total 750/2,982 allocated whole/temporal and 625/2,485 active after masking the baseball draw head. These are separate from gain counts.

Each plastic arm fits its surrogate for 16 epochs and retains epochs 4 and 16. Each checkpoint is then installed in its full graph, training/validation games are simulated, and a new decoder is fitted to those real responses. Selection minimizes equal-sport validation log loss; ties choose the earlier plastic epoch, then earlier decoder epoch. The surrogate score does not replace full-simulator validation. All scaling is fitted only on training rows, with temporal scaling separated by channel and window.

The fixed randomized comparison preserves IDs, annotations, signs, original self-edges, each neuron’s in/out degree and signed weight totals, contact-count histogram and gain-group sizes. Five deterministic sweeps propose directed double-edge swaps within equal contact count, source sign, exact KC source type (or non-KC) and exact MBON destination type (or non-MBON). Derived graphs are synthetic and separate from official data. A control with no changed edges or no changed plastic edges is invalid for topology conclusions. It cannot be silently replaced with another method.

Architecture selection retains the requirement for three complete seeds and uses mean equal-sport validation loss, breaking ties by fewer active parameters then variant ID. The paused matrix currently supplies no architecture with all three completed seeds, so this selection has not been delivered. The decoder-only diagnostic does not perform architecture promotion. The selected biological architecture’s three-seed probability ensemble becomes a separate shadow candidate; it does not become active automatically. Paired uncertainty resamples complete ISO calendar weeks within sport, including empty weeks, after averaging matched-seed per-game loss differences. The 2,000 percentile-bootstrap replicates use seed 20260910. Weekly blocks approximate but do not eliminate team/season dependence; seed spread is reported separately and three seeds do not become thousands of independent observations.

The implemented prospective workflow preserves the first valid pregame prediction per fixture revision and model, alongside a frozen feature-logistic forecast. Its first fixed cohorts are 100 eligible completed soccer fixtures and 1,000 baseball fixtures. Cohorts remain pending until real future games qualify; interim metrics are descriptive. No online training or automatic promotion is part of v2.

## V2 artifacts, inference and resumption

`make experiment-v2` is the original runner entry point; the current instruction explicitly forbids resuming it. Any future authorized resume must honor the recorded execution cancellation amendment. Cancelled jobs remain cancelled, and paused unfinished work is not counted as completed. Each experiment freezes the source arrays and game rows, split IDs, resolved protocol and its SHA256, graph-array hashes and a source-code snapshot. Its atomic `manifest.json` tracks jobs, actual phase/row progress, timestamps, errors and allowlisted artifacts. One registry writer lock excludes duplicate runners. Interrupted running jobs return to the queue. Every completed job’s registered result and saved artifact hashes are validated before any outer resume skip; a result committed before its registry update can be recovered without refitting. A completed experiment’s registered artifacts are checked on resume as well. See [the durable v2 protocol](EXPERIMENT_V2.md) for the required local inputs.

The original `source/code` freeze stays unchanged. Each runner invocation records its actual Python/C++ source hashes and snapshot under `executions/<execution-id>/`, and executing jobs retain that execution identity. Resume requires the core neural and fitting implementations (`brain.py`, `lif.cpp`, `learning.py`, `grouped_learning.py`) to match the original freeze. Changes to orchestration remain attributable to the execution that actually ran them; provenance is not rewritten to pretend an earlier invocation used later code.

Simulation-cache identity includes the feature array and its shape/type, graph arrays, expanded gains, stimulus seed, window layout and neural source hashes. Initial caches store pooled windows and raw KC windows; trained-graph caches retain pooled readouts and activity summaries. Whole and temporal readouts share the same four-window simulation pass. Row arrays are flushed before progress is recorded at approximately five-second intervals or phase completion. A completed cache must pass its stored array hashes before it is reused.

Each immutable candidate comprises `candidate/bundle.json` and `parameters.npz`. The bundle records schema version, variant, seed, graph identity, layout/window boundaries, neural source hashes, protocol hash, selected decoder epoch and parameter hash. Arrays contain expanded gains, training-only input and final-readout scaling, and decoder weights/bias. `load_candidate(bundle_path).predict_proba(features)` accepts one unscaled 16-feature row, verifies its saved identities and executes the full simulator. It applies the saved log1p/readout scaling in the same window order as evaluation; the surrogate never serves shadow forecasts.

The final report and all exported prediction rows are downloadable through the Training registry. The report contains seed measurements and aggregates, paired intervals, null invariant reports, failures and a development-comparison decision. Keep detailed tables in that immutable report. A biological-minus-null interval entirely below zero supports a benefit only for its named architecture, sport and split. A zero-crossing interval is uncertain; an entirely positive interval favors the comparator.

After selection, a local `output/v2-shadow.json` can freeze three bundle identities, the feature-logistic identity and activation time. The separate shadow ledger captures the first eligible forecast per fixture revision and model, checks the fixture again after inference and excludes forecasts saved at or after the actual cutoff. Changed start times require a new revision forecast; cancelled fixtures are void and unresolved results stay pending/held. Each sport’s first qualifying cohort freezes its membership once its threshold is reached. The first qualifying completion timestamp remains stable for cohort ordering. Current result eligibility and corrected outcomes are still rechecked: invalidated members are excluded from metrics and counted explicitly, without replacing them with later fixtures. A frozen membership therefore does not mean stale outcomes are permanently scored. There is no automatic promotion to active v1.

## Observatory interpretation

The canvas samples actual annotated soma positions and a strong-connection subset; it is not a reconstruction of axons, mushroom-body lobes or neuropil surfaces. Node classification is resolved by body ID. Cyan triangles identify sensory ALPNs, violet circles KCs, amber diamonds MBONs, light-gray squares other annotated cells and muted-gray crosses unknown categories. Ordinary connections are slate and supported plastic KC→MBON connections rose. A white firing halo retains the category color. Display filters and rotation do not alter computation.

Rich explanations are maintained in [brainExplainers.ts](../web/src/brainExplainers.ts) and available through labeled controls and pinned inspectors. The [guide](FLY_GUIDE.md#read-the-observatory) explains their use. A connectome is not a saved animal’s complete memories or behavior, and neither more visible cells nor more firing establishes greater intelligence. Missing annotations are unavailable; the interface does not infer cell function from activity or position.
